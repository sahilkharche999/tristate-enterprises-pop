Note 7 — Significant Assumptions

The accompanying forecast reflects the following significant assumptions:

  • Reserve study basis: the Replacement Fund forecast is grounded in the reserve study prepared by {{ static_data.reserve_study_expert_name }} dated {{ reserve_study_snapshot.study_date }}. Major components, their useful lives, and their remaining lives are taken from that study without adjustment.
  • Replacement-cost inflation: projected component replacement costs are increased at an annual rate of {{ '{:.0%}'.format(static_data.replacement_cost_increase_rate) }} over the prior-year cost.
  • Interest income: interest income on Replacement Fund cash and certificate-of-deposit balances is forecast at an after-tax annualized rate of {{ '{:.1%}'.format(static_data.interest_rate_after_tax) }}.
  • Income tax provision: a provision of ${{ '{:,.0f}'.format(static_data.income_tax_provision_estimate) }} is reflected in the {{ fiscal_year }} forecast for federal and state income tax on Replacement Fund interest income.
  • Assessment level: regular monthly assessments are forecast at the current rate of ${{ '{:,.2f}'.format(static_data.monthly_assessment_per_unit_current) }} per unit per month.
  • Funding plan schedule: the funding plan adjusts the per-unit Replacement Fund contribution per the Board's adopted schedule, applied piecewise across the 30-year horizon described in Note 6.

All assumptions reflect management's good-faith estimates as of {{ static_data.letter_date }} and may be revised as new information becomes available.

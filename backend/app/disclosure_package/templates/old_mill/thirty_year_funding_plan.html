Funding Plan Methodology

The 30-year forecast applies the following methodology, consistent with California Civil Code §§ 5550 and 5570:

  • Each year's beginning cash balance is the prior year's ending cash balance.
  • Replacement revenue for each year combines (a) the per-unit monthly Replacement Fund contribution multiplied by the unit count and 12, and (b) projected interest income on the prior-year-end cash balance at the after-tax interest rate documented in Note 7.
  • The per-unit monthly contribution is adjusted annually per the funding-plan schedule documented in Note 6.
  • Replacement expenditures are drawn from the reserve study component-replacement schedule prepared by {{ static_data.reserve_study_expert_name }}, with replacement costs inflated at the rate documented in Note 7.
  • The percent-funded ratio for each year is computed as the end-of-year cash balance divided by the cumulative estimated liability, rounded to whole percent half-even.

Material Risks and Sensitivities

The forecast is sensitive to material variation in the assumptions documented in Note 7. A 1-percentage-point change in the after-tax interest rate would change the projected ending cash balance by approximately ${{ '{:,.0f}'.format(static_data.bank_cd_balance_for_interest / 100) }} per year of forecast horizon. A 1-percentage-point change in the replacement-cost inflation rate would change the projected total replacement expenditures over the 30-year horizon materially. The Board reviews these sensitivities annually and may recommend updates to the funding plan in light of updated reserve-study findings.

Reference: California Civil Code §§ 5550, 5570. The Board reviews this trajectory annually and may revise the funding plan in response to updated reserve-study findings or material changes in operating costs, replacement-cost inflation, or interest-rate assumptions.

Section 4: Definitions and Calculation Basis

"Major component" as used herein means a major component identified by the reserve study expert under California Civil Code § 5550, having a remaining useful life of 30 years or less and an estimated repair, restoration, or replacement cost that materially affects the Reserve Fund.

"Estimated cash reserves" means the cash and cash-equivalent balance held by the association in its Replacement Fund accounts as of the last day of the prior fiscal year, as reflected in the books and records of the association.

"Estimated liability" for major component replacement is calculated for each component as replacement_cost × (useful_life − remaining_life) / useful_life, summed across all major components identified in the reserve study (§ 5565). Whole-dollar half-even rounding is applied to each component before summation; the policy mirrors the reserve-study extractor used in member-facing budget tools.

"Percent funded" is the ratio of estimated cash reserves to estimated liability, expressed as a whole percent. Per the California Department of Real Estate guidance, a percent-funded value of 70% or greater is considered "well funded"; values below 30% are considered "under funded."

"Under-funded balance" is the arithmetic difference between estimated liability and estimated cash reserves. The per-unit under-funded balance divides this difference by the number of separate interests in the association.

Section 5: Method of Calculating Funds for Replacement

The association funds its Replacement Fund through monthly assessments, projected interest income on bank and certificate-of-deposit balances, and the funding plan adopted by the board pursuant to Civil Code § 5550.

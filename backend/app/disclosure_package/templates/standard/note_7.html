Note 7 — Significant Assumptions

The accompanying forecast reflects the following significant assumptions:

{% set assessments_vary = matrix.recipient_grain == 'group' or matrix.recipient_grain == 'unit' %}
  • Reserve study basis: the Replacement Fund forecast is grounded in the reserve study prepared by {{ hoa_settings.reserve_study_expert_name }}{% if reserve_study_snapshot.study_date %} dated {{ reserve_study_snapshot.study_date }}{% endif %}{% if hoa_settings.reserve_funding_plan_date %}, with a funding plan dated {{ hoa_settings.reserve_funding_plan_date }}{% endif %}. Major components, their useful lives, and their remaining lives are taken from that study without adjustment.
  • Replacement-cost inflation: projected component replacement costs are increased at an annual rate of {{ '{:.0%}'.format(hoa_settings.replacement_cost_increase_rate) }} over the prior-year cost.
  • Interest income: interest income on Replacement Fund cash and certificate-of-deposit balances is forecast at an after-tax annualized rate of {{ '{:.1%}'.format(hoa_settings.interest_rate_after_tax) }}.
  • Income tax provision: a provision of ${{ '{:,.0f}'.format(computed.income_tax_provision) }} is reflected in the {{ fiscal_year }} forecast for federal and state income tax on Replacement Fund interest income.
  • Assessment level: {% if assessments_vary %} regular monthly assessments vary by ownership interest as shown in the assessment schedule. {% else %} regular monthly assessments are forecast at the current rate of ${{ '{:,.2f}'.format(computed.monthly_assessment_per_unit_current) }} per unit per month. {% endif %}
  • Funding plan schedule: the funding plan adjusts the per-unit Replacement Fund contribution per the Board's adopted schedule, applied piecewise across the 30-year horizon described in Note 6.

All assumptions reflect management's good-faith estimates as of {{ today }} and may be revised as new information becomes available.

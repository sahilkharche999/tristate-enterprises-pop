Note 3 — Basis of Presentation

The forecasted statement is presented in accordance with guidelines established by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) for the presentation of financial forecasts. Revenues and expenses are recorded under the accrual method when earned or incurred, respectively. The Association maintains separate fund-balance accounts for the Operations Fund (which collects assessments allocated to day-to-day operating expenses) and the Replacement Fund (which collects assessments and interest income allocated to the future replacement of major components identified in the reserve study).

Inter-fund transfers, if any, are recorded in the period in which the Board approves the transfer. The Association applies the disbursements method to its Replacement Fund for forecasting purposes, recognizing reserve expenditures only as the underlying replacement work is performed.
